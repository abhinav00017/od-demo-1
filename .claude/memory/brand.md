Logo: "only" in a purple script, "devs" in a dark near-black geometric sans, a purple full stop after it. Source file: `public/brand/logo-source.png` (1600x738, with background). Tagline: "Making business easy and stress-free."

Colours, sampled from the logo on 20 Oct 2026:
- Purple (primary, accent, the dot): #6F32D6
- Dark (headings, "devs"): #14272F
- Grey text (tagline, body): #757E82
- Background: very light cool grey, #F2F7F8 at top fading to #EAF2F3. Use #F5F8F9 flat or the gradient for hero areas. White #FFFFFF for cards.

Fonts: the logo wordmark is custom. For UI use a clean geometric sans close to the "devs" letterforms. Pick: "Plus Jakarta Sans" or "DM Sans" for headings and body. No serif.

Tone: minimal, simple, straight. One accent colour. Big type, lots of white space. The purple dot can repeat as a small motif (list bullets, active states).

**How to apply:** Every page in od-demo-1 uses these tokens as CSS variables. Demo client sites (the fictional clinic, cafe) have their own palettes; the hub chrome stays onlydevs. See [[decisions]].
