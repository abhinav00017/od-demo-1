od-demo-1 is the sales demo hub for onlydevs, Abhinav's business that sells digital services to local businesses. Started 20 Oct 2026 at `~/passion-projects/od-demo-1/`. Remote: https://github.com/abhinav00017/od-demo-1.

Flow: pick a client type (Clinic, Cafe) → see that type's services → click a service → a working demo. Abhinav shows this to prospects.

Siblings: `~/passion-projects/onlydevs_prod_1` holds the real clinic website template and client one, Dr. Gowthami (live at https://drgowthami.vercel.app). The hub's clinic website demo is a separate fictional clinic, not her site.

**Why:** Prospects must see every service working before they buy. Real client data must stay out of demos.
**How to apply:** Every service card must land on a page that looks finished, even if the real demo is not built. See [[decisions]] and [[services_pitch]].
